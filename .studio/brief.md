# Project brief — Portfolio (GoHighLevel & Marketing Automation Specialist)

_Date:_ 12 Nov 2026 · _Status:_ confirmed by client, with open items at the bottom

## Purpose
- Win clients and remote roles as a GoHighLevel / marketing-automation specialist.
- Success metric: qualified inquiries through the contact form or email, and interview invitations.

## Audience
- **Business owners** who need leads captured, followed up and booked without manual work. They are deciding: *can this person make my follow-up run itself?*
- **Marketing and digital agencies** needing reliable GHL implementation support. Deciding: *does he know GHL deeply, and is he dependable?*
- **Hiring managers** for remote automation roles. Deciding: *real experience, clear thinking, good communicator?*
- Device: mostly desktop for agencies and hiring managers; mobile for owners arriving from LinkedIn or referrals.

## Positioning
- Category: GHL freelancers / "VAs". The category's convention is the virtual-assistant template: headshot, skills grid, logos of tools, stock "automation" graphics.
- Difference: he builds **systems** (workflows, pipelines, reminders, call handling) that keep running after hand-off, and can explain the thinking behind them.
- Feel: premium, modern, technical. Must never feel generic, and must not read as "a typical virtual assistant template".

## Primary action
- **Start a project conversation** (inquiry form). Secondary: email / LinkedIn, view case studies.
